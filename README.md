
# EduPulse 360° | Education Business Intelligence Suite

### Turning Education Data into Business Insights

## 📊 Project Overview

EduPulse 360° is an interactive Power BI business intelligence project designed to analyze education institute performance across executive reporting, sales and admissions, student placements, and financial operations. It transforms business data into meaningful insights through KPI tracking, trend analysis, and performance comparisons.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall institute performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze sales leads, admission conversions, and lead source effectiveness.
- Evaluate student placement performance and salary metrics.
- Track revenue, fee collections, outstanding fees, and payment methods.
- Compare branch performance and identify business trends.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

## 📈 Dashboard Modules

### 1. Executive Overview
- Revenue, student, and admission KPIs
- Monthly revenue and admission trends
- Branch-wise revenue comparisons
- Course-wise student distribution
- Placement performance overview

### 2. Sales & Admission Analytics
- Lead generation and conversion analysis
- Lead source performance
- Branch-wise admission comparisons
- Monthly and yearly admission trends
- Sales and admission performance indicators

### 3. Placement Analytics
- Total placed students and placement rate
- Salary and placement performance metrics
- Company-wise placement distribution
- Placement-related calls and performance indicators

### 4. Fees & Financial Analytics
- Revenue and fee collection tracking
- Outstanding fees and pending payments
- Collection efficiency analysis
- Payment mode comparisons
- Financial performance monitoring

### 5. Integrated Performance Reporting
- Consolidated business performance indicators
- Comparative analysis across key metrics
- Trend monitoring for business performance
- Insights to support operational decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 💡 Key Business Value

EduPulse 360° demonstrates how interactive dashboards can help education institutes monitor business performance, understand admission trends, evaluate placement outcomes, track financial health, and make informed decisions using data.
